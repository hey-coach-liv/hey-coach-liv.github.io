myStory

I earned 10 university offers. Now I help students earn theirs.

I'm Olivia, a National Scholar at Western University studying Economics and Honours Business Administration at the Ivey Business School on a full-ride merit scholarship. I work across business, technology and law.

I graduated as Valedictorian of a 500+ student class at Bayview Secondary School with the IB Diploma and a 99% average, then received 10 offers from programs like Ivey AEO, Queen's Commerce, Rotman Commerce and Waterloo CS + Business. Over the last 3 years I've mentored students through that same process, and 30+ have prepared for scholarship interviews with me. That work became Olivia's University Studio.

Away from applications, you'll find me on a volleyball court. I've played and coached competitively for years, and coaching students feels a lot like coaching a team: a clear plan, honest feedback and a lot of reps.
